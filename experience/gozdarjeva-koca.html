Gozdarjeva koča (Forester’s hut)

📍 Slovenia · Remote🗓 Jan 2023 - Jun 2023Part-time

Gozdarjeva koča (Forester’s hut) is a boutique hut near an ancient Roman spa, deep in the forests of Slovenia. I ran its social media, turning a quiet getaway into a story worth following.

What I did

  • Managed the social media platforms, engaging with customers and influencers to promote the vacation house.
  • Collaborated with influencers, companies and the local municipality to create engaging content and grow brand awareness.
  • Ran monthly reporting to track social-media performance and make data-driven decisions.
  • Provided customer support to ensure a positive experience for guests staying at the house.
