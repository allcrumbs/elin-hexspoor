Public Fundraising Manager

FjordCleanUp · Oslo nonprofit

📍 Oslo, Norway · Remote🗓 Aug 2025 — Jan 2026 · Seasonal

FjordCleanUp is a Norwegian nonprofit cleaning trash straight out of the Oslo fjord — diving into the sea every Sunday, and rewarding volunteers with burgers and a sauna. I ran its public funding: finding the money that keeps the work going.

What I did

  • Identified and applied for public grants (municipal and national).
  • Monitored upcoming funding opportunities and aligned concepts with the right financial instruments and the organisation’s priorities.
  • Worked with management to plan funding strategies supporting long-term development and community-impact goals.
  • Secured municipal and other support to ensure access to critical infrastructure for the NGO’s operations.
