TEDx Speaker

TEDx University of Ljubljana

Ljubljana April 2025

In April 2025 I spoke at the 13th TEDx University of Ljubljana event, “Robovi neznanega” (Edges of the unknown). My talk, “Feel-good Addictions: how games can help us save the planet,” makes the case that we can make doing good as engaging as scrolling, through design, behavioural science and play.
