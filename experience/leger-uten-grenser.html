Fundraiser

Leger Uten Grenser · Doctors Without Borders (MSF)

📍 Oslo, Norway🗓 2026 - PresentField fundraising

After moving to Oslo, I took to the streets and doorsteps for Doctors Without Borders, turning a two-minute conversation with a stranger into a committed monthly donor for one of the causes I care about most.

The work

Direct-dialogue fundraising is sales at its most human: no script can carry you, only genuine connection. I represent MSF face to face, explain the work honestly, handle scepticism with warmth, and ask people to commit, every day, with a smile.

  • Acquire new regular donors through door-to-door and street dialogue.
  • Explain MSF’s medical humanitarian work and answer tough questions on the spot.
  • Build trust and rapport with strangers in minutes, then close the ask.
  • Stay resilient and positive across a full shift of rejections and yeses.
